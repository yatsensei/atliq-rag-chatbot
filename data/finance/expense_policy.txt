AtliQ Corp Corporate Expense Policy
All corporate expenses must have a clear business purpose. For client dinners or business travel, meals are capped at $75 per person per day. Receipts must be provided for all transactions over $15.
Travel requires prior approval from a department head if the estimated cost exceeds $500. Flights should be booked in economy class unless the flight duration exceeds 8 hours. Absolutely no personal expenses may be charged to an AtliQ Corp company card under any circumstances.
