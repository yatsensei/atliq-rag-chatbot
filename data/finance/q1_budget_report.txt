AtliQ Corp Q1 2025 Financial Report
The first quarter of 2025 showed strong financial performance. Total Q1 revenue reached $4.2M, exceeding expectations by 5%. The Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) was $1.8M, resulting in a healthy gross margin of 57%. 
Operating expenses remained within budget. Marketing spend for the quarter was $320K, primarily focused on the new enterprise software launch. Total headcount costs, including salaries and benefits, amounted to $1.1M.
