AtliQ Corp Parental Leave Policy
AtliQ Corp is committed to supporting our employees as they grow their families. Under this policy, employees designated as the primary carer are entitled to 12 weeks of paid parental leave. Employees designated as the secondary carer are entitled to 4 weeks of paid leave. 
All parental leave is paid at 100% of the employee's base salary. To be eligible for this paid leave, an employee requires a minimum of 6 months of continuous tenure at AtliQ Corp prior to the commencement of the leave.
