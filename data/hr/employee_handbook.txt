AtliQ Corp Employee Handbook
Welcome to AtliQ Corp. Standard working hours are from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM in the employee's local time zone, Monday through Friday. We operate on a hybrid model; remote work is permitted up to 3 days a week, pending manager approval. 
Code of Conduct: We expect all employees to foster an inclusive, respectful environment. Harassment or discrimination of any kind is strictly prohibited and grounds for immediate termination.
Performance Review Process: Formal performance reviews occur bi-annually in June and December. These reviews determine annual salary adjustments and bonus eligibility. Promotions are generally considered during the December review cycle.
