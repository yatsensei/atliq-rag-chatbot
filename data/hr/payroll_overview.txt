AtliQ Corp Payroll Guidelines
Payroll at AtliQ Corp is processed on a semi-monthly schedule. Payments are executed on the 15th and the last day of each month. If a payday falls on a weekend or a public holiday, payroll will be processed on the preceding business day.
All salary payments are made via direct deposit to the employee's nominated bank account. Employees can view, download, and print their payslips at any time by logging into the secure HR portal.
