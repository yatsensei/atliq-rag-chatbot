AtliQ Corp Strategic Plan 2025-2026
CONFIDENTIAL - Executive Board Only
Our primary objective for the next 24 months is international expansion. We plan to expand operations into the Southeast Asia market by Q4 2025, establishing a regional headquarters in Singapore. 
Concurrently, R&D will launch 2 new product lines focused on AI-driven analytics. Our financial target is to achieve 40% year-over-year revenue growth. To fuel this rapid expansion and support the new product launches, we aim to raise a Series B funding round by Q3 2025.
